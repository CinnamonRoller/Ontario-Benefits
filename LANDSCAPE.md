# Existing tools (checked 2026-11-01, search results only, tools not tested by us)

| Tool | Who | What it does, per search results |
|---|---|---|
| [Find benefits and programs](https://www.ontario.ca/page/find-benefits-and-programs) | Ontario government | Filters many provincial programs and credits. Says it does not determine eligibility. |
| [Benefits Finder](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/finder/tool.html) | Government of Canada | Federal tool with an Ontario filter. |
| [Ontario Social Service Navigator](https://www.ontariossn.ca/) | Third party, unofficial | Screens for OW, ODSP, Canada-Ontario Housing Benefit, childcare subsidy. |
| [BenefitCheck.ca](https://benefitcheck.ca/) | Third party | Calculator covering federal and provincial programs. |
| Calculatorplus / Canada Calculators | Commercial sites | OW and ODSP estimates; one says it uses rate tables only and does not test assets. |

## Results (2026-11-01)

Our difference is asset testing, source quotes and honesty about uncertainty, not the payment arithmetic.
